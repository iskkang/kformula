VERDICT SYSTEM

Simple for shoppers. Strict underneath.

This is not a rumor board. Each verdict is meant to be instantly understandable while still reflecting the quality of the evidence behind it.

Different Formula

Official sources indicate meaningful formula differences or distinct sunscreen active systems between markets.

Same Formula

The brand explicitly confirms the formula is the same despite different SPF labeling, packaging, or market wording.

Market-Specific Product

The U.S. or regional item is presented as its own market product, not simply a relabeled Korean-market original.

Not Confirmed

When the official documentation is not strong enough, we say so instead of pretending to know.

METHODOLOGY

Evidence before aesthetics. Even on a beauty site.

The design should feel premium, but the standard stays conservative. We do not call something a reformulation just because the ingredient order looks slightly different.

We prioritize official brand pages, official regional market pages and authorized retailer documentation. Ingredient-order differences alone are not enough to call a formula different because labeling rules can vary by country.

This is shopping information, not medical advice. Always follow the directions on the exact product you own.

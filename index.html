WHY IT MATTERS

Same brand. Similar tube. Sometimes a different product.

Regional sunscreen rules, labeling requirements and brand-specific reformulations can create products that look almost identical online. K-Formula Check keeps the conclusion simple while showing the evidence behind it.

OUR STANDARD

Evidence before aesthetics. Even on a beauty site.

We prioritize official brand pages, official market pages and authorized retailer documentation. Ingredient-order differences alone are not enough to call a formula different because labeling rules can vary by market.

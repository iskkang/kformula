BEAUTY OF JOSEON

Daily Tinted Fluid: U.S. vs EU

Last verified: August 8, 2026

SAME FORMULA

Different SPF label, same formula.

Beauty of Joseon explicitly says the U.S. and EU versions of Daily Tinted Fluid Sunscreen are exactly the same formula. The brand attributes the different front-label SPF numbers to regional regulatory labeling rules and testing standards.

U.S. label

The brand says the U.S. version is labeled SPF 40 and follows applicable U.S. testing/labeling requirements.

EU label

The brand says the EU version is labeled SPF 30, despite being the same formula, because of how SPF results are categorized and reported in Europe.

Evidence

The official BOJ explainer directly answers whether the versions are different: it says they are the same formula and explains why the labels differ.

Official BOJ formula/label explainer

Why this matters: this is the reason K-Formula Check does not classify a product as reformulated merely because ingredient presentation or SPF labeling looks different.

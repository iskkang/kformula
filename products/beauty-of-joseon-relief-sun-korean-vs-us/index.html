Relief Sun Korean formula vs U.S. market sunscreens

The U.S. products are not just a relabeled tube of Relief Sun. Beauty of Joseon currently presents its U.S. sunscreens as distinct market products using U.S.-style sunscreen active systems.

MARKET-SPECIFIC PRODUCT

The U.S. BOJ sunscreens are not simply relabeled Relief Sun.

Beauty of Joseon describes Day Dew as a sunscreen made for the U.S. market using FDA-defined active ingredients, and its U.S. Daily Relief formula also lists traditional U.S. OTC sunscreen actives. That is materially different from treating the original Korean Relief Sun as the same product with a U.S. label.

What shoppers mean on the Korean side

Relief Sun: Rice + Probiotics became known as the Korean SPF50+ PA++++ product. Shoppers usually mean this original when they ask for the “Korean formula.”

  • Sold through Korea and Asia-focused channels

Current U.S. BOJ sunscreen products

What matters on the regional side

Beauty of Joseon’s U.S.-market sunscreen products use their own product names and U.S.-style active ingredient structure.

  • Day Dew is explicitly described as made for the U.S. market
  • Daily Relief lists avobenzone, homosalate, octisalate and octocrylene as actives
  • Different product names are an immediate clue

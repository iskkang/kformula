Relief Sun Korean formula vs U.S. market sunscreens

Last verified: August 8, 2026

MARKET-SPECIFIC PRODUCT

The U.S. products are not just a relabeled tube of Relief Sun.

Beauty of Joseon describes Day Dew as a sunscreen made for the U.S. market using FDA-defined active ingredients, and its U.S. Daily Relief formula also lists traditional U.S. OTC sunscreen actives. That is materially different from treating the original Korean Relief Sun as the same product with a U.S. label.

Original Korean-market intent

Relief Sun: Rice + Probiotics became known as the Korean SPF50+ PA++++ product. U.S. shoppers continue to specifically search for the “original Korean formula.”

  • Different market/regulatory presentation
  • Often sourced from Korea/Asia-based retailers
  • Do not assume a U.S.-retail BOJ sunscreen is the same tube

Current U.S. BOJ sunscreen examples

Day Dew is explicitly described by the brand as made for the U.S. market. Daily Relief lists avobenzone, homosalate, octisalate and octocrylene as active ingredients.

  • Day Dew: U.S.-market product
  • Daily Relief: U.S. OTC-style active ingredient panel
  • Different product names are an important clue

Evidence

Beauty of Joseon — Day Dew: the official FAQ states that Day Dew is made for the U.S. market and identifies its FDA-defined active ingredients.

Official Day Dew product page

Beauty of Joseon — Daily Relief: official product page lists U.S.-style active sunscreen ingredients.

Official Daily Relief product page

Interpretation rule: this result does not mean every non-sunscreen BOJ product has a different U.S. formula. Ingredient-label order alone is not sufficient evidence of reformulation.

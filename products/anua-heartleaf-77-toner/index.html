WHO IT MAKES SENSE FOR

Best viewed as a simple soothing layer.

It makes the most sense for someone who wants a light, low-friction toner step and values comfortable layering. It makes less sense if you expect one toner to create an obvious acne, pore, or pigmentation transformation.

  • Better fit: oily, combination, sensitive-feeling routines
  • Less compelling: very dry skin needing rich hydration
  • Expectation to avoid: dramatic standalone results

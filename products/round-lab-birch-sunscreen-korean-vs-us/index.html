Birch Juice Sunscreen: Korean vs U.S. UVLock

Last verified: August 8, 2026

DIFFERENT FORMULA

The U.S. UVLock version is a market-specific reformulation.

Round Lab's official U.S. materials describe Birch Moisturizing Sunscreen UVLock SPF 45 Broad Spectrum as an OTC-registered sunscreen developed to retain the benefits of the original Birch Juice sunscreen while meeting SPF 45 Broad Spectrum regulations in the U.S., and call it exclusive to the U.S.

Korean original

The original Birch Juice Moisturizing Sunscreen is the Korean-market product behind the brand's popularity. Shoppers often seek it specifically for its Korean sunscreen formulation.

U.S. UVLock

Official U.S. product: Birch Moisturizing Sunscreen UVLock SPF 45+ Broad Spectrum. Round Lab states this is an OTC registered U.S.-exclusive version developed for U.S. regulations.
